# Learner guide

## Complete a workshop

1. Open the direct lab link supplied by your instructor.
2. Read the assignment and open the evidence pack. Download the fictional source pack if useful.
3. Complete the five tasks in sequence. All editable table cells in the current task are required before the task can be marked reviewed.
4. Cite document and clause IDs. Distinguish a supplied fact, inference, assumption and legal-research question.
5. Review your work against the source, tick the review statement, then use **Review & continue**. Editing a reviewed task returns that task to draft.
6. Complete the three-question knowledge check at the end. Feedback supports revision; it does not certify professional competence.
7. Compare your reasoning with the discussion themes and quality checklist.
8. Use **Export submission** for a readable Markdown file. Send that file through the channel your instructor specifies.

## Save and restore

- The browser stores typed responses, quiz selections, review markers and an optional alias locally. There is no account, central server or automatic submission.
- Export a **JSON backup** regularly. Keep it before changing browser/device or clearing site data.
- On the Learning Portfolio page, choose **Restore backup**. Review the listed matching labs before replacing their local answers.
- A lab reset asks for confirmation and affects only that lab in this browser. Exported copies are unaffected.
- On a shared browser profile, other users may see saved work. Use an alias and avoid personal or confidential information. Reset local work after retaining your own export if required by your instructor.
- Browser saving may be unavailable in private browsing, restricted environments or after a storage error. The app warns when it cannot save. Export your work before leaving.

## AI tools

Every activity can be completed manually. Lab 3 includes intentionally flawed simulated AI output for offline practice. If your instructor permits an external AI tool, use an approved environment and only the fictional source materials. The app itself does not call an AI service. It does not need a key or subscription.

## Scores and legal research

Risk-row scores are likelihood × impact; bands are teaching priorities. Weighted vendor comparisons require weights totalling 100 and scores from 1 to 5. A high score never overrides a legal or security blocker. Quiz and completion records are learning aids, not legal approvals.

Bhutan, EU and UK rules must be assessed separately. Verify current primary texts, jurisdictional scope, relevant facts, amendments, commencement and deadlines. The official-source desk provides research starting points, not a consolidated legal opinion.

## Accessibility and printing

Use the skip link and keyboard navigation. Source packs open with native disclosure controls. Tables can scroll horizontally on small screens. All inputs have accessible labels. Export Markdown for the full response text; printing a current page can truncate long editable fields depending on the browser.
