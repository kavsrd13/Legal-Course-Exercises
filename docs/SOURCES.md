## Official research starting points

Source-link review date: 6 November 2026. This is not a certification of current consolidated law. Live access was checked for the European Commission AI Act page, the NSB framework page and the NIST framework page. The RMA notice was returned in official-site search results, but a direct fetch timed out. EUR-Lex may require an interactive browser challenge. Other links are official research starting points and require a delivery-time check.

### Bhutan: official legislation repository

- URL: https://www.legislation.gov.bt/
- Status: Primary legislation — confirm text, amendments, scope and commencement
- Teaching boundary: Teaching scenarios do not establish the currently applicable Bhutanese rule. Check the actual Act and sector requirements.

### WIPO Lex — national IP law research

- URL: https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/
- Status: Official IP legal research portal
- Teaching boundary: Verify national ownership, assignment, novelty and registration rules. Portal indexing is not a legal opinion.

### European Commission: AI Act

- URL: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai
- Status: Official explanatory guidance — law has conditional scope
- Teaching boundary: Check intended use, role, territorial scope, classification and current dates against applicable legislation. GPAI is a separate layer; no fixed dates are embedded in the lab.

### OECD AI Principles

- URL: https://oecd.ai/en/ai-principles
- Status: International policy principles
- Teaching boundary: A governance reference, not automatically a directly binding Bhutanese law.

### NIST AI Risk Management Framework

- URL: https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework
- Status: Voluntary risk-management framework
- Teaching boundary: Use as a control-design reference; distinguish voluntary practice from a binding duty.

### RMA: regulatory stance on crypto currency, 30 April 2025

- URL: https://www.rma.org.bt/view_news/106/
- Status: Official regulatory notice — verify current position
- Teaching boundary: Research lead for the regulatory perimeter; recheck for newer instruments and distinguish RMA and GMC regimes. No token launch is authorised by this exercise.

### FATF: virtual assets

- URL: https://www.fatf-gafi.org/en/topics/virtual-assets.html
- Status: International standards and guidance
- Teaching boundary: Identify how domestic law implements standards and which participants are obliged entities.

### EU GDPR — official regulation text

- URL: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng
- Status: EU law — only where applicable
- Teaching boundary: Research Articles 3, 5, 6, 9, 28, 32–35 and Chapter V as relevant. UK law and transfer mechanisms require a separate check.

### Bhutan National Data Governance Framework

- URL: https://nsb.gov.bt/national-data-governance-framework/
- Status: Official framework — assess status and implementation
- Teaching boundary: Distinguish a governance framework from an Act or sector rule. Confirm the actual document version and relevant obligations.

### NIST Cybersecurity Framework

- URL: https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework
- Status: Risk-management framework
- Teaching boundary: Useful for governance and response planning; it does not itself establish a universal breach-notification deadline.

## Claims deliberately avoided

- A universal legally mandated AI liability cap or unconditional reimbursement of every fine.
- Automatic high-risk status for every legal tool, or treating a downstream user as a GPAI model provider by default.
- A blanket statement that GDPR/UK GDPR is Bhutanese law or applies solely because a supplier is located there.
- Treating SCCs as required for every movement of data or sufficient without the rest of the transfer analysis.
- Assuming labels prove privilege, payment proves IP ownership, a token proves asset title, or pending licensing authorises launch.
- One universal incident-notification deadline across regulators, insurers and contracts.
- Treating GNH values, international guidance or a company policy traffic light as a statutory rule.
